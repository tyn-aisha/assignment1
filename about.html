Inside the studio

Girl's Girl opened in a converted print workshop in central Astana, and the building's bones are still visible in the exposed brick and steel beams. The floor is split into a rig zone for barbell work, a quiet mat zone for pilates and mobility, and a ring for boxing pads. Every wall is ventilated for winter training, because the studio stays open through the coldest months of the year. Coaches plan each block around the group in the room that day, so no two Tuesdays look the same. It was designed to feel like a friend's apartment that happens to have a squat rack in it.

How the studio grew

  1. 2021 — Girl's Girl starts as a Saturday pop-up class in a rented dance hall.
  2. 2022 — The first permanent studio opens with two coaches and one class format.
  3. 2023 — Boxing Flow and Reformer Pilates are added to the schedule.
  4. 2024 — Membership passes 400 active members.
  5. 2025 — A second training room opens for recovery and mobility work.
  6. 2026 — The studio launches its first online booking and progress-tracking system.

A few things worth knowing

  • Every coach is certified in strength training and in basic sports first aid.
  • Classes run in Kazakh, Russian and English, depending on the coach and the room.
  • The studio keeps a lending rack of belts, wraps and grips for anyone who forgets theirs.
  • Saturday mornings are reserved for a free open floor session for new members.
