A women-only studio in Astana

Train like the room is already yours.

Girl's Girl is a strength and movement studio built for women, by women. No judgment, no mirrors-only culture — just fifty-minute classes that leave you stronger than you walked in.

Our classes

Every class is capped at twelve people, so your coach actually knows your name and your knees.

  • Strength Circuit — barbell and dumbbell fundamentals
  • Reformer Pilates — control, posture and core work
  • Boxing Flow — pad rounds set to a full-length playlist
  • Mobility & Recovery — stretching, breathwork and rolling

How membership works

  1. Book a free tour of the studio and meet a coach.
  2. Choose the plan that matches how often you want to train.
  3. Come to your first class — mats, weights and towels are provided.
  4. Track your progress with your coach every four weeks.
